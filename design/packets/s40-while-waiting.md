## 2. Rulings (the hq sitting's, reversible)

1. The template gains `## While waiting` between `## Handover` and `## Next act`, seeded with the one line `- none`. Its lines are one of three shapes: `- ready: <row> (<repo>, S<n>)`, an off-path row that can be built without a gate, at most three; `- owed: <item>; <what it needs>`, an owed item that needs no gate (a runsheet, a refuter round, a draft); `- none`, alone. `PARTS` gains the section and `check_text` requires it only in a charter that has it or that was opened by this version: a charter without the section (opened before alpha.23) passes `check_text` and gets one warning, `no While waiting section (opened before alpha.23): add it with - none`. A line of another shape, or `- none` beside other lines, is a warning naming the line.
2. `warnings()` gains the red: a Next act that `waiting()` reads as WAITING (n of 1 or more, or 0 misshapen) while the section holds any line other than `- none` yields the line `WAITING while ready: <the first ready or owed line>; take it, since WAITING is legal only when While waiting reads - none`, and `charter check` exits 1 (a check said no) with it; the meter wait (111-117) stays exempt and returns first. `close` is unchanged by the red (it runs `check_text` alone). `charter.warnings` returns the red first in its list; `tests/test_charter.py` reads it by the phrase `WAITING while ready`.
