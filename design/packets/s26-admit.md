## 2. Rulings

1. **Observe only.** `mode` takes `observe` (the default); `enforce` is refused by the config reader as not yet built (S27a) and read as `observe`. In this packet nothing is ever refused: the module always calls `next(e)`.
2. **The fit and the peak** are pure functions over the ledger's rows (readings, spawns, `done` lines), with `now` passed in, per §3.2. Intervals of 10 minutes with a reading at each end and no reset between give r; n_f counts helpers in flight over the interval. Least squares by the normal equations (stdlib). Fewer than 20 intervals: the seeds. The family is read from `model`, else `parentModel` (note 8): `opus`, `sonnet`, `haiku`, `fable`, else `other`. L_t is the median of `done` durations for type t (`subagentType`, a fork as `fork`), the seed's life when none; a running helper older than its type's 95th-percentile life is counted done (note 4's mean-life-left is S4). Decide the fine grain yourself inside §3.2 and log each choice in one sentence.
3. **The seeds** (reversible, replaced by P3): `a` 0.10 points a minute for each active main thread (a session with a reading in the last 45 minutes); `b` opus 0.15, sonnet 0.05, haiku 0.02, fable 0.25, other 0.15; life 15 minutes for every type. `session.admission` is `{mode, ceiling, margin, seeds, ceiling_7d}` with defaults `observe`, 97, 3, the seeds above, and 97; an unreadable field is its default, as `rules.parts` does.
4. **The verdict**, in this order: no five-hour reading → `unread`; the newest reading's `resetsAt` passed → `stale` (`wait` while another spawn admitted under a stale reading is in flight, else `admitted`); seven-day `percentUsed` ≥ `ceiling_7d` → `denied`; peak + margin > ceiling → `deferred` (the session's `start` line says interactive) or `not-started` (`-p`); else `admitted`. Each carries §3.3's text, word for word, with the reset as local `HH:MM` and the window's figures filled in; the deferred text's "by HH:MM at the latest" is the reset plus one minute.
5. **`admission admit`.** `claude-session admission admit --session <id>` reads one JSON object on stdin (`subagentType`, `model`, `parentModel`, `background`, `fork`, `tool_use_id`, `rateLimits`, `resume`), checked as S25's lines are (M2's shape checks); inside the one lock it reads the ledger, decides, appends one `verdict` line `{ts, session, tool_use_id, verdict, mode, peak, P, T, family, type}`, and prints `{"verdict", "mode", "text", "peak"}`; exit 0, 2 refused, 3 lock. It never refuses a spawn itself; the module decides what to do with the answer.
6. **The module's call.** `agent.spawn` calls `admit` before `next` and SendMessage's `tool.call` calls it with `resume: true`, bounded and caught as S25's calls are; in this packet the answer is logged and `next(e)` always follows; S25's `spawn` and `resume` records stay as they are.
7. **Version** 2.0.0a12 in the four places, `test_doctor`'s literal with it.
8. **Tests.** `tests/test_admit.py`: one test per verdict rule in ruling 4 with its text pinned; the peak against a synthetic day (readings every 5 minutes, helpers of two families starting and ending, a reset) within 1 point of a hand computation; the fit recovering known `a` and `b` from synthetic intervals; the seeds below 20 intervals; the 95th-percentile age-out; the config defaults and an unreadable field; `enforce` read as `observe`; `admit`'s `verdict` line and its refusals writing nothing. `admission.test.ts`: `admit` called before `next` on a spawn and on a SendMessage resume, `next(e)` unchanged whatever `admit` answers (`deferred`, `denied`, a rejection, a hang). Before the report, `claude plugin test plugin` passes every case and `claude plugin validate plugin` reads no error.
